# Clinical review checklist

**Current status: NOT CLINICALLY APPROVED.** The starter information and keyword-based escalation rules are demonstration materials only.

Before any participant-facing pilot, a suitably qualified maternal-health clinical lead with relevant Nigerian practice experience should:

1. Review every knowledge-base entry against current Nigerian national guidance and relevant WHO recommendations.
2. Record reviewer name, credentials, source/version, review date, scope and approval status for each item.
3. Review pregnancy, childbirth, postnatal, newborn and mental-health content separately.
4. Validate the plain-language wording and ensure it does not imply diagnosis or individualized treatment.
5. Expand and clinically validate escalation scenarios, including false-negative and ambiguous cases. A phrase match is not a comprehensive screening system.
6. Review the urgent-care instructions and verify local emergency and referral pathways for the pilot location.
7. Define escalation to human care and procedures for clinical incident reporting, correction, rollback and content updates.
8. Approve the product's user notices, limitations, privacy language and intended-use statement.
9. Confirm applicable regulatory, ethics, safeguarding and data-protection requirements.
10. Document formal sign-off and acceptance criteria before any user-facing pilot.

Do not describe this prototype as clinically validated, diagnostic, treatment-providing or ready for patient care until the above work is completed and documented.
